# Camera capture

Tickets 11-12: photograph one accessible meter and build reproducible crops
with labelled expected values. Record display type, units, decimal placement,
resolution and examples of unreadable/transition frames.

Tickets 22 and 24: compare mounting angle, distance, focus, glare and lighting.
Keep private images/account details out of Git; local captures go in ignored
`captures/`. Only sanitised, consented fixtures may be explicitly reviewed
for inclusion elsewhere.

Saved photos drive the PC OCR tests. Select capture frequency independently
of reporting frequency, considering changing displays and low-balance alerts.
